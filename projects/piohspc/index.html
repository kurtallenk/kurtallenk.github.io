About PIOHSPC

Established occupational health and safety expertise across the Pacific.

The Pacific Institute of Occupational Health & Safety Professional Consultancy provides HSE consulting, training, and compliance services to organizations across the Pacific region. Since 2016, our work has centered on one goal: helping organizations build safer, more productive working environments.

We do this through modern solutions and global best practices combining hands-on audits and management systems work with practical training and responsive incident support, so that safety programs hold up in the field, not just on paper.
