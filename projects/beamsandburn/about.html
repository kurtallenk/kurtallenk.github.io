About Us

Craftsmanship, finished by precision

We're Beams & Burn, your go-to destination for high-quality laser engraving. Whether you're looking for custom gifts, personalized home decor, or business branding, we bring your ideas to life with precision and artistry.

Our Story

Started from a small workshop, we combine laser tech with craftsmanship to produce quality engraved products. We focus on detail, sustainability, and fast turnaround.

Our Values

  • Quality-first
  • Eco-conscious sourcing
  • Customer collaboration

How We Work

  1. Send your artwork or brief.
  2. We prepare a mockup for approval.
  3. Confirm material and size.
  4. We engrave and finish your piece.
